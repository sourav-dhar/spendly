Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and a securely hashed password. As you use the Service, we also collect the financial data you choose to log — including expense amounts, categories, descriptions, and dates. We do not ask for or store payment card numbers, bank account details, or any other sensitive financial identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Service. This includes displaying your expenses back to you, generating category breakdowns and summaries, and allowing you to filter transactions by date range. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or marketing partners. We may use aggregated, anonymized data to understand general usage patterns and improve Spendly.

3. Data Storage

Your account information and expense data are stored in a secure database. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using industry-standard algorithms. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, but no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of third-party services for essential functionality, such as web hosting and font delivery. These providers may process limited technical information (such as IP addresses) as part of delivering their services. We do not integrate with advertising networks or analytics trackers that profile individual users. Any future integration with third-party services will be disclosed in an updated version of this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or if you would like to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.example. We will do our best to respond to all reasonable requests in a timely manner.
